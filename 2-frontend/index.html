How to Play

Slide. Bonk. Don't fall. Shove rival soaps out of the arena: a sink, a toilet or a bathtub. You're out when the center of your body crosses the edge.

Laptop: WASD / arrow keys to move, Space to dash, E to parry.
Phone: joystick on the left, big DASH and PARRY buttons on the right. Dash recharges in 3 seconds.

Parry: tap it right before a soap rams you (it works for a split second). Their push bounces back at them and they get dizzy. If you miss it recharges in 4 seconds, if you land it it is back in 2.

Your soap: tap the soap next to your name (or Change soap in the lobby) to pick a shape. It is just for looks, every soap plays the same.

The arena starts shrinking after 15 seconds. Every round begins with a 3-second countdown. In the sink, watch the faucet: a white stripe on the water warns where the next water jet will shove you. The faucet shuts off for good once the arena starts shrinking.

Scoring: last soap standing = 3 points. If the 45 seconds run out with several survivors = 1 point each. Everyone falls at once = draw, 0 points. Eliminated? You join the cheering crowd: press CHEER (Space) to cheer the rest on, then you're back next round.

After 3 rounds the highest score wins. A tie means a shared victory.

Bots: Quick Play starts straight away against 3 bots. In Play with Friends the host can add bots to empty seats (up to 8 players in total). Bots follow the same rules and scoring as everyone else, and a friend joining a full lobby takes a bot's seat.

Use Back (or the exit button during a match) to leave a room at any time.
