How to Play

Slide. Bonk. Don't fall. Shove rival soaps out of the arena: a sink, a toilet or a bathtub. You're out when the center of your body crosses the edge.

Laptop: WASD / arrow keys to move, Space to dash, E to parry.
Phone: joystick on the left, big DASH and PARRY buttons on the right. Dash recharges in 3 seconds.

Parry: tap it right before a soap rams you (it works for a split second). Their push bounces back at them and they get dizzy. If you miss it recharges in 4 seconds, if you land it it is back in 2.

Your soap: tap the soap next to your name (or Change soap in the lobby) to pick a look. 18 to choose from, including a hooded space knight, a masked dark lord, a young blade-wielding hero, a little blue-and-white droid and a crew from the reef. Just for looks, every soap plays the same.

The arena starts shrinking after 15 seconds. Every round begins with a 3-second countdown. In the sink, the toilet and the bathtub, watch the faucet: it is alive. It slithers out of a hole in the rim, picks a victim and glares at them while a white stripe on the water warns where the jet will shove you. It keeps spitting every few seconds for the whole round, even while the arena shrinks.

Scoring: win the round (last soap standing) = 3 points. Every kill = +1 point: knock a rival out and you get it if you were the last one to bump them (within 3 seconds, parry rebounds count too). Falling on your own or from the faucet alone gives nobody a kill. If the 45 seconds run out with several survivors = 1 point each. Everyone falls at once = no win points, but kills still count. Eliminated? You join the cheering crowd: press CHEER (Space) to cheer the rest on, then you're back next round.

After 3 rounds the highest score wins. A tie means a shared victory.

Ready: in a friends room everyone except the host taps I'm ready in the lobby. The host can start once all of them are ready (bots are always ready). Tap it again to take it back. Everyone has to get ready again after a match.

Bots: Quick Play starts straight away against 3 bots. In Play with Friends the host can add bots to empty seats (up to 8 players in total). Pick Easy, Normal or Hard for the bots (on the home screen, in the lobby, or after a match). Bots follow the same rules and scoring as everyone else, and a friend joining a full lobby takes a bot's seat.

Use Back (or the exit button during a match) to leave a room at any time.
